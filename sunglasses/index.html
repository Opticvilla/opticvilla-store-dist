Choose sunglasses for Indian sun, travel, and daily wear

Start with protection, then choose the style. Look for sunglasses that clearly state their UV protection, sit securely at the bridge, and cover the eyes without touching the cheeks. Polarized lenses can reduce reflected glare during driving and outdoor use, while lens colour mainly changes visual comfort and appearance.

Compare frame width, bridge fit, temple length, material, and lens shape before ordering. Lightweight frames are useful for longer wear, while larger lenses can provide broader coverage. Check the individual product page for measurements, available colours, delivery information, and return eligibility before completing your purchase.
