Choose eyeglasses that fit your face, prescription, and day

A comfortable pair of eyeglasses starts with the right measurements. Compare lens width, bridge width, and temple length with a frame that already fits you. Consider lightweight materials for all-day wear, flexible hinges for convenience, and a frame shape that leaves your eyes centred within the lenses.

Your prescription and routine should guide the lens choice. Single-vision lenses suit one viewing distance, while other prescriptions may need specialist lens options. Check each product page for frame measurements, material, colour, lens compatibility, delivery information, and return eligibility before ordering.
